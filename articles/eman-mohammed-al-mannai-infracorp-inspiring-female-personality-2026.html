The Most Inspiring Female Personality to Watch in 2026: Eman Mohammed Al Mannai

How a certified international real estate expert and trainer turned two decades of disciplined leadership, constant learning and belief in others into a pioneering career in Bahrain's property sector.

The Empire Insight Official Cover Feature
The Empire Insight Editorial Board October 20, 2026 • Spotlight: Eman Mohammed Al Mannai (Certified International Real Estate Expert & Trainer | Head of Sales, Infracorp) • Real Estate & Leadership
Executive Spotlight 2026

Behind every skyline is a person who believed it could exist before the first foundation was poured. In Bahrain's real estate sector, that person is often Eman Mohammed Al Mannai. A certified international real estate expert and trainer, and Head of Sales at Infracorp, she has spent more than two decades turning ambitious developments into places where people live, work, and invest. In 2026, her story is more than a career record. It is a case study in what disciplined leadership, constant learning, and genuine belief in other people can build.

Two Decades Shaping Bahrain's Skyline

Born in 1980 in the city of Muharraq, Eman built her foundation at the University of Bahrain, where she earned a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration and Marketing. That blend of business strategy and marketing instinct became the signature of her career. With over 20 years of experience across the real estate markets of Bahrain and the Arabian Gulf, she has been part of landmark projects across the Kingdom, overseeing more than 15 freehold developments, 17 residential and commercial towers, and over 7,000 apartments and luxury villas.

Numbers like these describe scale, but they also describe trust. Developments of this size demand sound strategy, clear communication, and teams that deliver under pressure. Leading key development initiatives, Eman has become known for combining strategic acumen with a real understanding of what buyers and investors need. Her work drives high-impact real estate developments and strategic advancements, both locally and internationally.

Her strength lies in connecting the technical side of property with the human side of decision making. Buyers rarely purchase square meters alone. They purchase security, status, and a future, and Eman has built her sales leadership around that difference. Her marketing background helps her tell a project's story with clarity, while her valuation expertise keeps every promise grounded in numbers.

“ I never see a project as a building. I see it as a promise to the people who will live in it and the investors who believed in it. — Eman Mohammed Al Mannai, Head of Sales, Infracorp

A Career Built on Firsts

In a sector long dominated by men, Eman has repeatedly been the first. In 2015, she became the first Bahraini woman to earn the Certified International Property Specialist (CIPS) credential. She is also a Certified Real Estate Trainer through the University of Cambridge, a Certified Real Estate Valuation Specialist through the Arab American Academy for Real Estate Professionals, and a licensed real estate practitioner and valuer with Bahrain's Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA). A Certified Professional Coach and Trainer, she brings the same rigor to developing people that she brings to developing property.

Her reach extends well beyond Bahrain's borders. Eman was among the first female keynote speakers hosted by the Saudi Chambers of Commerce to encourage cross-border investment into the Kingdom of Bahrain, a platform that placed her at the center of regional economic conversation. Recognized across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region, she stands as a pioneering female leader in her sector, and her presence embodies the empowerment of Bahraini women in executive leadership.

“ Being the first is never the goal. The goal is to make sure I am not the last. — Eman Mohammed Al Mannai

Beyond the Boardroom: Author, Mentor, Entrepreneur

Eman's influence does not stop at the sales floor. She serves as Chairperson of the Real Estate Committee at the Bahrain Entrepreneurship Organization (BEO) and as an advisor to the Good Word Society through Madd Al-Insaniyah for Volunteer Work. Her professional memberships also include the Real Estate and Construction Committee of the Bahrain Chamber of Commerce and Industry, the Bahrain Foundation for Training and Human Resources, and the Bahrain Real Estate Development Association (BaPDA).

She is also a storyteller. In 2019 she published Property and Life in 60 Days, the first specialized book to combine real estate education with personal growth, written to teach readers about property while motivating them to develop themselves. In 2024 she followed it with A Person May Be Born Twice, an account of her professional and inspirational life journey. Her entrepreneurial portfolio has grown as well, with the launch of her signature luxury fragrance line, the Eman Al Mannai Collection.

“ Real estate taught me that value is built over time, and life works the same way. Whether it is a tower or a person, the foundation decides everything. — Eman Mohammed Al Mannai

Recognition That Reflects Influence

The industry has taken notice. Eman received the Top Sales Performance Award in 2024 and was named Woman of the Year in Real Estate and Construction the same year. Her influence across business and social platforms has earned her a place, for three consecutive years, on Gulf Executive Magazine's list of influential women in Bahraini society.

Yet the awards tell only part of the story. What sets Eman apart is the consistency of her approach: credentials earned, knowledge shared, and opportunities opened for others. She trains, coaches, writes, and mentors, treating expertise as something that grows when it is passed on. Her story also carries weight for younger women watching from the edges of industries that once felt closed to them. Each credential, keynote, and leadership role widens the path, and by sharing her journey openly through books, training, and public speaking, she turns personal achievement into shared possibility.

As Bahrain and the wider Gulf continue to attract global investment, leaders who combine technical mastery with vision will define the next chapter. Eman Mohammed Al Mannai is already writing hers, one development, one lesson, and one generation of professionals at a time. In 2026, she is, without question, a personality to watch.
