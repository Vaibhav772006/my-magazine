GTA 6 Release Date: November 19, 2026 Launch, Pre-Load, Price and What to Know

Confirmed date, platforms, pre-order prices and pre-load timing for Grand Theft Auto VI, plus what is still unconfirmed.

The Empire Insight Official Cover Feature
The Empire Insight Editorial Board November 3, 2026 • Gaming & Entertainment
Gaming Watch • November 2026

After two delays and years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto VI is now just weeks away. Rockstar and Take-Two have kept one date on the calendar since late 2025, and this month is when it finally arrives. Here is what is confirmed, what is still unknown, and how to get ready without falling for scams.

The Date That Matters: November 19, 2026

Rockstar Games set the release for Thursday, November 19, 2026, after moving it from its earlier May 2026 target. Take-Two has repeated the date in its financial updates since, and its leadership has publicly said it is not planning another slip. As of this writing there is no new delay announcement, which is a big deal for a game that has already moved twice.

Platforms: Console First, PC Still Unconfirmed

GTA VI launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no official PC date. Some reports point to a PC version in early 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed anything, so treat those dates as rumor. If you only play on PC, plan to wait and ignore any site that claims to sell an early PC key.

Pre-Orders, Pre-Load and Price

  • Pre-orders: Open now on the major console stores, with reported prices of $79.99 for the standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate edition. Prices can vary by region and retailer, so check your own store page.
  • Pre-load: Digital pre-loading is reported to begin on November 12 at local midnight, which lets you download in advance and play the moment the game unlocks.
  • Unlock time: Some outlets report a rolling local-midnight unlock on launch day. Confirm the exact time on your console store before you plan a late night.
  • Campaign first: The single-player story is the focus at launch. No separate release date has been announced for the online mode.

Rockstar Is Also Releasing an Album

Rockstar has said a 34-track official album will arrive on launch day alongside the game, and it has been rolling out merchandise in the weeks before. It is a sign of how big a cultural event this launch is being treated as, closer to a blockbuster film premiere than a normal game release.

“A big launch rewards the patient: pre-load early, buy from official stores, and trust only Rockstar's own announcements.— The Empire Insight Editorial Board

How to Get Ready Before November 19

  1. Free up storage now. Big open-world games need a lot of space, and pre-loads often require extra room for updates. Clear space before November 12.
  2. Pre-load on a wired connection if you can, and start early in the day so any problems show up before launch night.
  3. Check your console version. Only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are supported, not older consoles.
  4. Stay safe from scams. Expect fake giveaways, leaked-build downloads and cheap “keys”. Buy only from official stores or well-known retailers.
  5. Set expectations for kids. The series is for mature audiences, so set up parental controls if younger players share your console.

What We Still Don't Know

Details that remain unconfirmed in official channels include the exact file size, a PC release date, extra editions beyond those listed, and the shape of the online mode after launch. Anything beyond Rockstar and Take-Two announcements should be read as speculation.
