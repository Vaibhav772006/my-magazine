India vs Nepal Cricket: Players, Match Preview and Key Statistics

India A tours Nepal for three T20s on December 9, 11 and 13. Schedule, head-to-head record, recent form and players to watch.

The Empire Insight Official Cover Feature
The Empire Insight Editorial Board November 20, 2026 • Spotlight: India vs Nepal (T20 Series, December 2026) • Sports
Cricket Watch

Searches for India vs Nepal cricket are climbing, and the reason is simple: there is a fresh series on the calendar. Nepal will host an India A side for three T20 matches in December 2026 at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. Here is the schedule, how the two sides have met before, what Nepal's recent form looks like and which players are worth watching. One note up front: squads hadn't been announced when we checked, so we'll keep names to those who are established regulars.

India vs Nepal Match Schedule: The December T20 Series

The Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN) confirmed the three-match T20 series after talks with BCCI secretary Devajit Saikia during an ICC meeting in Edinburgh. The senior Nepali national team will play India A, which is India's second-string side made up of rising domestic players and fringe national squad members.

  • 1st T20: Wednesday, December 9, 2026, 9:45 AM IST
  • 2nd T20: Friday, December 11, 2026
  • 3rd T20: Sunday, December 13, 2026, 9:30 AM IST
  • Venue: Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur

Confirm timings and broadcast details on the official CAN and BCCI channels, since fixtures can change. Remember this is India A and not the full national side, so don't expect household names to line up.

Head-to-Head: How Have India and Nepal Met Before?

Senior meetings between the two have been rare, which is part of the appeal. In the 2023 Asia Cup, India beat Nepal in a rain-affected group game. At the 2023 Asian Games in Hangzhou, India won their T20 quarter-final, with Yashasvi Jaiswal scoring a century. In T20 internationals, that Asian Games result is the only meeting listed in ESPNcricinfo's records, and India won it.

The picture is more mixed in smaller formats. In the Hong Kong International Sixes in November 2025, Nepal beat India by 92 runs, a reminder that short games produce upsets. More recently, India Under-19s beat Nepal Under-19s by 10 wickets on November 6, 2026 in the DP World Men's U19 Asia Cup. Nepal were bowled out for 128 in 42.3 overs, and India chased it down in 19.1 overs without losing a wicket.

“On paper it is India versus Nepal. In reality it is the biggest stage Nepal's cricketers get against the biggest team in their region.— The Empire Insight Editorial Board

Nepal's Form and Growing Ambitions

Nepal has become one of the more ambitious associate teams in Asia. The side played at the 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup, where one of its group matches against England was staged at Mumbai's Wankhede Stadium. For many young Nepali players, a series against India A isn't just exposure. It's a chance to show selectors and franchise scouts they belong on the biggest stages.

Players to Watch

Final squads will decide this, but Nepal's regulars have been the core for several seasons. Keep an eye on batters such as Kushal Bhurtel and all-rounders such as Dipendra Singh Airee, who has built a reputation for fast finishing in T20s, as well as spinner Sandeep Lamichhane if he is selected. On the India A side, expect a mix of domestic stars and IPL-experienced young players. We'll update this guide once the squads are confirmed.

Key Statistics and Trends to Know

  • Format: three T20s spread over five days at a single venue, Kirtipur.
  • Recent trend: India's Under-19s beat Nepal comfortably this month, while Nepal has shown it can win in the shortest formats.
  • Upset potential: T20 games are decided by a couple of overs. A good powerplay or a hot spinner can flip a match.
  • Experience gap: India A's players are used to high-pressure domestic cricket, while Nepal's top players often balance international and franchise leagues.

Why This Series Matters Beyond the Scoreboard

Cricket's growth story in the region runs through fixtures like this. Regular games against Full Member nations raise standards, sell tickets and inspire the next batch of players. They also open doors for coaches, broadcasters and sponsors. The same logic applies in business, where underdogs grow by playing stronger opponents often.
