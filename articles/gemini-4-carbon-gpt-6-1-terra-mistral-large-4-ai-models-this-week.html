Gemini 4 Carbon, GPT-6.1 Terra and Mistral Large 4: The AI Models Launched This Week

A plain-English guide to the newest AI launches, staged access, pricing changes and the API key security warning developers should act on.

The Empire Insight Official Cover Feature
The Empire Insight Editorial Board November 3, 2026 • Technology & AI
AI This Week • Model Launches

The last week of October was packed with AI releases. Google unveiled Gemini 4 Carbon, OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6.1 Terra, and Mistral opened up a trillion-parameter model. The bigger story is that the race is shifting from “who is smartest” to “who is usable, affordable and safe.” Here is a plain-English guide to what launched and what to watch.

Google Gemini 4 Carbon: Powerful, but Gated

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, and it reportedly tops a widely followed independent AI index. The twist is access. Google is giving its strongest capabilities to cyber defenders first, with no date yet for general developer availability. Reported introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, rising later to $4 and $20. This staged approach is becoming the standard for models that are strong at finding software vulnerabilities, since the same skill can help attackers as well as defenders.

Separately, Google made its computer-use feature generally available on October 30, which lets AI agents operate software on a person's behalf.

OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra: Rolling Out

OpenAI started rolling out GPT-6.1 Terra on October 26 across its API, work products and coding tools. OpenAI also said ChatGPT and Codex usage limits will move to a token-based system in December, and it recently adjusted GPT-6 in ChatGPT to give shorter answers with less flattery. If you rely on these tools for work, watch how the new limits affect your plan.

Mistral Large 4: A Trillion Parameters, Open Weights

French company Mistral released open weights for Large 4, a model with about one trillion parameters. The license allows self-hosting and commercial use, but it reserves selling hosted access to the model as a service for a separate commercial license. If you plan to build a product on it, read the terms before you commit.

Other Stories Worth Knowing

  • Canada backs Cohere. The government announced up to C$1.6 billion for an equity stake, tied to job creation and domestic computing power.
  • Anthropic tools and plans. The company released a usage and cost analytics API in beta and renamed some personal plans.
  • Google puts ads deeper into AI search, and New York is setting new rules for AI chatbots and children.
  • Publisher pushback. Several major AI and tech firms back a shared robots.txt token that lets sites opt out of AI training.

A Security Warning for Anyone Building With AI

Security firm Wiz reported that nine AI developer platforms were breached in October, exposing stored environment variables that contained AI provider keys, and that stolen keys were tested within about 30 minutes. If you host projects on a platform that sent a breach notice, rotate your keys immediately.

  1. Rotate exposed API keys and revoke the old ones.
  2. Set spending limits and alerts on every AI provider account.
  3. Never commit keys to public repositories. Researchers recently found tens of thousands of live keys sitting in public code.
  4. Use separate keys per project so one leak does not expose everything.

How to Choose a Model Without Chasing Headlines

Match the model to the job. Use a cheaper, faster model for simple tasks and a stronger one only for hard problems. Test two options on your own real prompts. Check pricing carefully, since token costs and usage limits are changing quickly. And confirm that the model is actually available to you, because staged releases mean headline models are not always open to everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gemini 4 Carbon?

Google's newest top model, announced October 28, 2026, with early access going to cyber defenders first.

Is GPT-6.1 Terra available now?

It began rolling out on October 26 to OpenAI's API, work products and Codex, so availability may vary by account.

Can I use Mistral Large 4 commercially?

The license permits commercial use and self-hosting, but selling hosted access requires a separate license.

Why are labs limiting access to new models?

Strong cyber capabilities can be misused, so labs are releasing them to defenders first.

The winning AI model is the one that fits your task, budget and risk level.
