Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026: Date, Importance and Traditional Rituals

Tithi from 7:15 AM on Nov 20 to 6:31 AM on Nov 21. Dates, parana, puja vidhi, Tulsi Vivah and the meaning of the day Vishnu wakes.

Festival Guide

After four months of rest, Lord Vishnu wakes. That's the heart of Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026, also called Prabodhini Ekadashi, Devutthana Ekadashi or Kartiki Ekadashi. This year the Ekadashi tithi begins at 7:15 AM on November 20 and ends at 6:31 AM on November 21, so the Smarta tradition observes the vrat today while Vaishnava devotees observe it on November 21. Below you'll find the dates, the meaning, the step-by-step rituals and the Tulsi Vivah confusion explained in plain language.

Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026 Date and Tithi Timings

Timings below are for New Delhi, as published by Drik Panchang and repeated by several outlets. Your city can differ by a few minutes, so check a local panchang.

  • Ekadashi tithi begins: 7:15 AM, Friday, November 20, 2026
  • Ekadashi tithi ends: 6:31 AM, Saturday, November 21, 2026
  • Smarta observance: Friday, November 20
  • Vaishnava observance: Saturday, November 21
  • Parana (breaking the fast): after Hari Vasara ends at about 12:07 PM on November 21, with Drik Panchang listing a window of roughly 12:40 PM to 2:51 PM for Delhi. Those following the Gauna (second-day) Ekadashi break the fast on the morning of November 22, around 6:58 to 9:09 AM in Delhi.

Why two dates? Different traditions use different rules to decide which day counts when a tithi straddles two dates, so both are widely followed. If your family has always done it a certain way, keep to that.

Why Dev Uthani Ekadashi Matters

The festival falls on the eleventh day of the bright fortnight of Kartik. According to tradition, Lord Vishnu goes into cosmic sleep on Devshayani Ekadashi in the monsoon and wakes on this day, ending the four-month period called Chaturmas. Weddings and other auspicious ceremonies are traditionally avoided during Chaturmas, which is why this day is seen as the start of the wedding season.

In Maharashtra, the day is known as Kartiki Ekadashi and is especially significant to the Warkari tradition. Devotees travel to Pandharpur to honour Lord Vitthal, and the town fills with pilgrims. Across Maharashtra you'll hear its spirit in local bhajans and processions.

Dev Uthani Ekadashi Puja Vidhi: Step by Step

Customs vary by region and family, but here is a commonly followed outline.

  1. Wake early and bathe. Clean the house and the puja area. Many people draw rangoli, often with Lord Vishnu's footprints leading into the home.
  2. Take a sankalp. Resolve to observe the Ekadashi fast, either fully (nirjala) or with fruits, milk and water (phalahar).
  3. Set up the altar. Place an image or idol of Lord Vishnu, often with Goddess Lakshmi. Many families include a Shaligram stone.
  4. Offer worship. Light a diya, offer flowers, fruits, sugarcane and Tulsi leaves, and recite Vishnu mantras or the Vishnu Sahasranama.
  5. Wake the Lord. In many homes, people ring bells, beat plates and chant “Utho Dev, Utho Dev” to symbolically wake Vishnu.
  6. Read or hear the vrat katha and complete with aarti.
  7. Observe parana on time. Break the fast on Dwadashi, within the parana window, after Ekadashi has ended.

A common tradition is to avoid grains and rice on Ekadashi. Many families also avoid plucking Tulsi leaves on the day itself, so the leaves needed for worship are gathered the day before. If you're unsure, ask an elder or your family priest.

Tulsi Vivah 2026: Which Day Should You Choose?

Tulsi Vivah is the ceremonial wedding of the Tulsi plant with Lord Vishnu, usually represented by a Shaligram or an idol of Krishna. It begins from Dev Uthani Ekadashi and, depending on local tradition, can be held on Ekadashi, Dwadashi or up to Kartik Purnima. Several 2026 panchang reports point to November 21 as a popular date, while others list November 24 for Purnima. Both are accepted in different traditions.

Typical arrangements include decorating the Tulsi pot like a bride, building a small mandap from sugarcane stalks, applying turmeric and kumkum, offering a red chunri and singing wedding songs. Many families also share prasad with neighbours.

What Not to Do

  • Don't break the fast before the parana window.
  • Don't eat grains or rice if you're following the traditional vrat.
  • Avoid quarrels, harsh words and overeating, since the day is meant for calm and devotion.
  • If you have a medical condition, take advice before fasting. A fruit-based fast is a gentler option.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the date of Dev Uthani Ekadashi in 2026?

The Ekadashi tithi runs from 7:15 AM on November 20 to 6:31 AM on November 21, 2026. Smarta devotees observe it on November 20 and Vaishnava devotees on November 21.

What is the parana time for Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026?

For Delhi, parana is after Hari Vasara ends around 12:07 PM on November 21, with a published window of roughly 12:40 PM to 2:51 PM. Gauna Ekadashi followers break the fast on the morning of November 22. Check your local panchang.

Why is Dev Uthani Ekadashi important?

It marks Lord Vishnu's awakening and the end of Chaturmas, and the traditional start of the wedding season.

When is Tulsi Vivah in 2026?

Many panchangs mark November 21, though it can be held from Ekadashi until Kartik Purnima depending on local tradition.

Is Dev Uthani Ekadashi the same as Kartiki Ekadashi?

Yes. In Maharashtra it is widely known as Kartiki Ekadashi, with a major pilgrimage to Pandharpur.
